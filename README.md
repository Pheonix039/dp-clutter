## Overview
Ever get tired of being able to see what you're looking at when you play Minecraft? Well worry no more! Your ol' pal Wade's got just the thing for you!

Just brew my super awesome kick@$$ potion in one of those brewer thingies using a piece of rotting flesh to get my powers!

![Awkward Potion + Rotten Flesh = DP Potion](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b0ce82f028d56187686c0d472562da52493c8cf9.png)

Once you take a sip of my awesome brew, you'll give any losers nearby a screenful of Deadpool!

![Aww! Look at all the widdle Wades!](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f9c510c5a2e1c56eed29de9d919647978719d022.gif)

If you wanted the effect itself instead of the aoe for any reason, you can use that spider eye thingy to turn any kick@$$ potion into a PWNED potion!

![PWNED potion recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7f8d9c9b51d5835c4a56c87bcb62d05147d40d2e.png)

## Deadpool's Challenge
Only the coolest can take on my challenge! **/gamerule deadpoolsChallenge true** gives all hostile mobs my super cool effect, making sure you don't forget who's the best!
